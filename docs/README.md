# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes OctoAcme's approach to cross-functional projects, from validating an initiative through delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The process emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, transparent communication, and learning from outcomes.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme initiates work by confirming the business need, success measures, stakeholders, risks, resources, and readiness to proceed. Approved initiatives move into planning, where teams define a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, owners, milestones, a Definition of Done, and an initial test plan. During execution, teams deliver small increments, track work on a project board, and manage dependencies and risks.

The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes, priorities, and success measures; developers build and test the work; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular standups and delivery syncs surface progress and blockers, PM and Product Manager alignment keeps delivery coordinated, and weekly or milestone-based updates and demos keep stakeholders informed. Blockers escalate from team triage through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed.

Quality is supported by small reviewed pull requests linked to issues and acceptance criteria, automated tests and linting in CI, security scanning, and unit, integration, and end-to-end smoke tests as appropriate. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons into owned and measurable improvement actions.
