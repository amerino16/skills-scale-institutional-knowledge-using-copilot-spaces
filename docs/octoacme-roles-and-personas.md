# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners provide strategic direction and ensure a project delivers its intended business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and expected business value
- Secure or coordinate sponsorship, funding, and organizational support
- Make or escalate major scope, priority, and go/no-go decisions
- Review progress against intended outcomes and resolve sponsor-level blockers

### Collaboration
- Partner with the Product Manager to align outcomes and priorities
- Work with the Project Manager on major decisions, escalations, and milestone updates
- Provide business context and approvals to Developers and other delivery roles through the Product Manager and Project Manager

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical design and help ensure the solution is feasible, maintainable, and aligned with system architecture.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and document significant decisions
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and engineering dependencies
- Support estimation and sequencing of technical work
- Promote maintainability, reliability, and appropriate engineering practices

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers on implementation choices, reviews, and technical problem solving
- Partner with the Product Manager to assess technical trade-offs against product priorities
- Coordinate dependencies and communicate technical risks to the Project Manager
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and technical validation needs

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads coordinate quality practices and validation so that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and is ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test coverage and validation approaches for project work
- Coordinate functional, integration, regression, and other relevant testing
- Track defects and communicate quality risks and release readiness
- Promote clear, testable acceptance criteria and early quality feedback

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Coordinate with Developers to plan testability, resolve defects, and automate checks
- Align with the Project Manager on test progress, blockers, and readiness milestones
- Partner with Operations / SRE / Release Managers on release verification

---

## UX / Design Researchers

### Role Summary
UX or Design Researchers bring user needs and usability considerations into product decisions and solution design.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Develop interaction designs, prototypes, and usability guidance
- Consider accessibility and consistency in the user experience
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback

### Collaboration
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs, research insights, and product priorities
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to ensure designs are feasible and implemented as intended
- Coordinate with QA / Test Leads on usability and accessibility validation
- Share research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager for planning and stakeholder communication

---

## Operations / SRE / Release Managers

### Role Summary
Operations, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), or Release Managers help ensure services and changes can be deployed, operated, and supported reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness, deployment, monitoring, and rollback requirements
- Coordinate release activities and verify operational prerequisites
- Advise on reliability, capacity, and support risks
- Help prepare incident response and post-release monitoring

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to address operability and reliability requirements
- Coordinate with QA / Test Leads on deployment checks and post-deployment verification
- Align with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, risks, and stakeholder updates
- Advise the Product Manager on operational trade-offs that affect feature scope or rollout
